Appleland (Web3 marketplace for physical & virtual NFT assets)

  • RoleSystem Design / WEB 3.0 / NFT / Virtual currency
  • Year2023.07 - 2024.02
  • TagsWEB 3.0 · NFT · Virtual currency · WEB UI design · Gaming · AIGC

Overview

Appleland is the bridge between users, the platform and physical merchants — a full trading platform that helps businesses run across online and offline models, extending NFTs into the real economy. It also handles trading in purely virtual assets, with community and other services on the roadmap.

Project background

As Web3 stacks converge on the same technical ground, Appleland set out to break the wall between virtual and physical — a digital-intelligence bridge connecting users, the platform and real-world merchants. A full-scenario trading protocol lets physical merchants run online and offline as one business, moving NFTs from digital collectibles toward certificates of real-world entitlement.

Core redesign

Working to the business requirements, we fed experience issues, user surveys and usage data back into the work: refining layout, reworking interaction logic, tightening the details, and lifting retention. Design effectiveness was judged on survey satisfaction, community feedback and backend user data.

The refinement path

  1. Surface (visual design)
  2. Framework (interface layout)
  3. Structure (interaction flow)

My Role

Led the visual rebuild of the Appleland marketplace from the ground up. Mapped Design Tokens to front-end logic and kept delivery above 95% design fidelity. Designed a stepped feedback system for on-chain latency, giving explicit progress instead of a stalled screen. Routed image production through AIGC, cutting the asset cycle by 50%.
