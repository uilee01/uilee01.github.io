XiaoWo Accessibility Call Assistant

Letting deaf and hard-of-hearing people “hear” a phone call (1M+ users / ITU-D case study)

  • RoleLab Project / Mobile design / Accessibility / WeChat mini program
  • Year2020.01 - 2023.05
  • TagsMobile design · Accessibility · WeChat mini program · Senior-friendly · Hard of hearing · China Unicom XiaoWo

Overview

The product reaches more than 1,000,000 hard-of-hearing users across mainland China, and the work was selected for an ITU-D international report. The Changting King Card line I led became an in-house benchmark: recognised by the China Disabled Persons’ Federation and by China Unicom’s XiaoWo team, with 800,000+ core active hard-of-hearing users.

Setting user feedback against usage data is what gives us a reliable read on where to steer the design.

Correction needs to be read against the actual scenario. Survivorship bias is real: the people who answer a survey tend to be the ones who already like the product. Those who don’t never fill anything in. So once feedback is collected, it goes into a requirements pool and gets triaged first. One case where research and analysis pulled in opposite directions: early on we used coarse location, following national information-security guidance. That left people feeling unsafe when sharing their location or making an emergency call, because the position came out too far off. Working within the rules, we changed the approach so location sharing is both compliant and precise enough to actually be useful.
